About关于我

Hi, I’m Yuhan. You can call me Zuzu.你好，我是朱语涵，也可以叫我 Zuzu。

I work on game programming and technical audio, using C++, Unreal Engine, Unity, Wwise and Python. Outside development, I play piano and take part in photography and video projects.我做游戏编程和技术音频，使用 C++、Unreal Engine、Unity、Wwise 和 Python。开发之外，我弹钢琴，也参与摄影与视频创作。

Study学习

Game Engineering游戏工程

I have completed my Game Engineering coursework and dissertation at the University of Warwick and am awaiting my degree award. My dissertation project uses Unity and Python to generate music from game state.我已完成华威大学游戏工程的课程和毕设，目前等待学位授予。毕设使用 Unity 与 Python，根据游戏状态生成音乐。

Technical audio技术音频

Audio in the game游戏里的声音

In BallDarkEcho, I connect Wwise events to movement, weapons and abilities through C++ and Blueprint. In Anti-Alien, I worked on Blueprint music switching, ambience and interaction sounds.在 BallDarkEcho 中，我通过 C++ 和 Blueprint 把 Wwise 事件接入移动、武器和技能。在 Anti-Alien 中，我参与了 Blueprint 音乐切换、环境音和交互音效的实现。

Working style工作方式

Small steps, regular playtests逐步实现，随时试玩

I test changes in the game, track bugs and keep notes for the next iteration. When working with a team, I also help organise tasks and bring the group's work together.我会把改动放进游戏里测试，记录问题，再继续调整。团队合作时，也会参与任务安排和小组内容整合。

Outside development项目之外

Piano, photography and video钢琴、摄影与视频

I enjoy playing piano and arranging anime music, along with photography and cosplay. My video work includes camera work on 《雪舞华裳》 and filming and editing a Reze / Chainsaw Man short.我喜欢弹钢琴、改编动漫音乐，也喜欢摄影和 Cosplay。影像作品中，我参与了《雪舞华裳》的拍摄，以及 Reze / Chainsaw Man 短片的拍摄与剪辑。
