About关于我

A personal site, with game work at its core.这是个人主页，游戏作品是它的一部分。

I am Yuhan Zhu, also known as Zuzu, currently studying Games Engineering at the University of Warwick. I enjoy making game systems more readable and responsive through code, sound, and careful player feedback.我是朱语涵，也使用 Zuzu 这个名字，目前在华威大学学习游戏工程。我喜欢通过代码、声音和细致的玩家反馈，让游戏系统更清晰、更有响应感。

Study学习

Games Engineering游戏工程

I am working across C++, Unreal Engine, Unity, gameplay systems, and interactive audio. My current dissertation work explores real-time adaptive game music using a Unity and Python workflow.我正在学习和实践 C++、Unreal Engine、Unity、玩法系统与交互式音频。当前毕业设计探索使用 Unity 与 Python 工作流实现实时自适应游戏音乐。

Technical audio技术音频

Sound with a game purpose有玩法目的的声音

I am particularly interested in spatial audio, game-state-driven music, and the practical bridge between authoring tools and in-engine behaviour. Wwise and FMOD are tools I use to explore that bridge.我尤其关注空间音频、由游戏状态驱动的音乐，以及音频制作工具与引擎内行为之间的实际连接。Wwise 与 FMOD 是我用来探索这条连接的工具。

Working style工作方式

Make the evidence visible让证据可见

I prefer small implementation steps that can be observed, tested, and explained. That means keeping builds, notes, screenshots, and clear constraints alongside the code—not only a final claim that something works.我更喜欢能够被观察、测试和解释的小步实现。因此我会保留构建、笔记、截图和明确的限制条件，而不只是在最后声称“它能运行”。
